Can I arrange a regular clean?+
We plan to offer regular home and workplace cleaning, as well as one-off cleans and guest changeovers. Frequency and availability will be agreed around your property.

YOUR INFORMATION
A simple privacy note.
When you send an enquiry, your name, email address, optional phone number and cleaning requirements are sent to Formspree for processing and passed to A Squared Cleaning. Formspree stores submissions in its dashboard and sends email notifications to our business inbox. We use these details to respond to your enquiry and discuss your cleaning requirements.
You can contact us about your information at ASquaredCleaning@outlook.com. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your cleaning requirements.
The website does not save form entries in browser storage. If you choose “Copy my enquiry”, your enquiry and contact details are copied to your device’s clipboard. Choosing the email link opens your email app; you decide whether to send that email.
The hosting service and form provider may process technical information needed to provide and protect their services. Read Formspree’s privacy policy for details about its processing.
